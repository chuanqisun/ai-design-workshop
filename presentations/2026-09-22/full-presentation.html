// 13 . METHODOLOGY // STEP 1

Generate: Combinatorial Divergence & Grounding

Quantity over quality: forcing wider creative apertures

CERAMICS EXPERIMENT

Art & Fear (1993): Students graded purely on volume of clay produced higher quality pots than students who spent all term polishing one.

COMBINATORIAL EXPANSION

Combinatorial parameters force AI to explore extreme feature boundaries instead of generic median cluster traps.

GRANOLA GROUNDING & REVERSE PROMPTING

• Raw sketches + audio $\rightarrow$ interactive study guides.
• Reverse prompting / self-interview to defeat writer's block.
• Algorithmic literature review pipelines.

GENERATIVE DEMOS mugen.ai (Demo) CryptoPunks Art & Fear (1993) Granola Grounding Annotated Bib (Local HTML) Lecture Notes (Local HTML)
  • Art & Fear: The famous pottery class grading experiment: volume of production leads to technical mastery.
  • Combinatorial Exploration: Parametric attributes (CryptoPunks) prevent models from falling into bland local minima.
  • Granola Grounding: Lecture audio + hand sketches $\rightarrow$ interactive study explainer.
  • Reverse Prompting: Having the model interrogate the designer to extract implicit knowledge.
// 14 . METHODOLOGY // STEP 2

Evaluate: Quantification & Virtual Prototyping

The Double Diamond convergence: converting qualitative ideas into sortable metrics

CLASSICAL ROOTS

Alex Osborn (Applied Imagination, 1953) & J.P. Guilford: Divergent exploration must be anchored by rigorous convergent filtering.

EVALUATION TECHNIQUES
  • Rubric Quantification: Prompt AI to score criteria (feasibility, novelty) to enable numeric ranking.
  • Semantic Distance: Project ideas onto polar axes (practical vs speculative) using embeddings.
  • Interactive Data Viz: Let LLMs write quick web graphs of huge solution landscapes.
FEATURED HARDWARE METHOD

Virtual Prototyping: Time Travel Radio

Validating physical device behaviors, audio feedback, and teleabsence interactions via software simulators before cutting hardware.

EVALUATION TOOLS Answering Machine (Demo) Prism by Linus (Thesephist) A Notebook for Seeing with LLMs Applied Imagination (1953)
  • The Convergence Imperative: Divergence without evaluation is just noise. Double Diamond requires convergence.
  • Quantification: Computers excel at numbers. Turn qualitative descriptions into scores to sort 500 ideas.
  • Semantic Distance: Measuring cosine similarity against polar opposites to visualize idea distributions.
  • Virtual Prototyping: Time Travel Radio app built for Hiroshi Ishii's Tangible Media Group class.
